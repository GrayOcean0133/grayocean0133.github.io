Why AI-Link 为什么选择我们
01

双博士导师指导 Two PhDs Co-Advising

张汉成博士、胡圆圆博士共同指导，亚琛工业大学学术背景，研究覆盖计算机视觉、生成式 AI、智能监测机器人全链条。 Co-advised by Dr. Zhang Hancheng and Dr. Hu Yuanyuan, both with RWTH Aachen academic background — covering computer vision, generative AI, and intelligent monitoring robots end-to-end.

02

真实工程项目 Real Engineering Projects

已孵化「路愈者 RobotHealer」道路病害检测系统，入围挑战杯，在江西、江苏、贵州、浙江多地试点应用。 We've incubated "RobotHealer", a road-distress detection system — a Challenge Cup finalist, currently piloting across 4 provinces (Jiangxi, Jiangsu, Guizhou, Zhejiang).

03

高性能 AI 算力 High-Performance Compute

主力训练服务器 rs630：4 × NVIDIA RTX A6000，合计 192 GB 显存。项目立项后可申请使用，支撑大规模模型训练与生成式 AI 微调。 Main training server rs630: 4× NVIDIA RTX A6000, 192 GB total VRAM. Available upon project approval — supports large-scale training and generative-AI fine-tuning.

04

软硬件一体实践 Full-Stack Hardware + Software

DJI Avata 360 无人机 + PCB 设计调试设备 + 第二台 RTX 3080 工作站，面向立项项目开放，从算法到嵌入式装备全栈打通。 DJI Avata 360 drone + PCB design/debug bench + a secondary RTX 3080 workstation, open to approved projects — from algorithms to embedded equipment.
